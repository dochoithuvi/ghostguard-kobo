Profile approval does NOT enable touch blocking. Approval starts a Shadow-only
probation period. After the required probation sessions, the profile becomes
PROTECT_ELIGIBLE=1 for a future Protect Beta release, while PROTECT_ACTIVE stays 0.

License validation is fail-closed: an invalid/missing license prevents the
observer from starting. Input handling remains fail-open: GhostGuard never grabs
or blocks the touchscreen in this release.
